# 🌍 CarbonTrack: The Magic Notebook

Imagine the Earth is a giant, beautiful garden. Every time we do things like ride in a car, leave the lights on, or throw away food, we accidentally leave a little bit of invisible "yucky smoke" in the air. 

**CarbonTrack** is like a **magic notebook** that helps us keep the garden clean! 

When you tell the magic notebook what you did today—like "I rode in a car for 10 miles"—it uses its math skills to tell you exactly how much "yucky smoke" that made. By keeping track of this every day, the notebook helps us learn how to make less smoke so the earth stays happy and green! 🌳🌎

---

## ✨ The New Magic Features We Just Added!

Here are the cool new things we just built into your magic notebook:

### 📖 The Smart Rule Book (Dynamic Emission Engine)
Before, the notebook only knew a few simple rules for counting the smoke. Now, we gave it a giant, smart rule book (a database)! This means if scientists learn new things about how much smoke a car makes, we can easily update the rule book without having to take the notebook apart.

### 🛑 The Spelling Checker (Validation)
Sometimes people might accidentally type silly things, like "I drove minus 10 miles" or they forget to write what they did. We added a friendly helper that checks their math and says, *"Oops! That doesn't make sense, please fix it before we write it in the notebook!"*

### ⚙️ Your Secret Settings (User Preferences)
Just like decorating your own room, you can now customize your notebook! We added a special page where you can tell the notebook if you want to measure things in "Kilometers" or "Miles", and you can decide if you want to keep your Earth-saving goals a secret or share them with friends.

### 🌟 Shiny Sticker Prep (New Database Tables)
We got the notebook fully ready to start handing out shiny stickers (Badges) and setting big targets (Goals) so we can celebrate when you do a super good job taking care of the planet!
